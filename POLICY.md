# Policy — player churn predictor

**Trust label:** `educational-reference`  
**Updated:** 2026-10-30

## In scope

- Pure weighted risk score (recency · frequency drop · consistency · depth)
- Soft-fail Hyperion activity sample → profile → score
- Demo at-risk / active profiles

## Out of scope

- Guaranteed churn forecasts
- Sole input to bans or airdrops
- Full 30-day archived analytics product

## Weights (locked teaching)

Recency 35% · frequency drop 30% · consistency 25% · depth 10%
